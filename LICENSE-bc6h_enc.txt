The other BC6H packer of this crate, of signed and unsigned blocks (src/bc6h_enc.rs and
src/bc6h_enc_tables.rs), is ported from bc6h_enc.h (https://github.com/0xc0de/bc6h_enc), which
is the BC6H encoder of Microsoft's DirectXTex made into one file by Alexander Samusev. These are
the two notices that file carries for it. (Its third, for a conversion between floats and
halves by Mike Acton, is for code that is not ported.) The port itself is offered under the
Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE and NOTICE).
